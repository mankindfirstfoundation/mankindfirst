How we spend, in practice

  • Every distribution is documented. Beneficiary lists (kept confidential), receipts, photos and GPS points are filed for every activity.
  • Two-signature spending. No payment moves without authorisation from two office bearers.
  • Community verification. Local committees confirm deliveries before any activity is counted in our reports.
  • Zakat ledger. Zakat-designated gifts are tracked separately and spent only on verified zakat-eligible households.

Report a concern

Seen something that doesn’t match our standards? Email safeguarding@mankindfirst.org (confidential, read only by the safeguarding lead) or use the contact form marked “Safeguarding”. Every concern gets a written response within 7 days.
